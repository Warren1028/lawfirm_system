Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to set up?

Setup typically takes about 2 weeks. We handle the full integration with your CMS (Smokeball, Clio, etc.), customize your intake forms, and train your team to ensure a smooth launch.

Can I use it with my existing CMS?

Yes! WKO Intelligence works with 10+ case management systems including Smokeball, Clio, MyCase, Practice Panther, and more. If your CMS has an API, we can integrate with it.

Do clients need special software?

No. Clients fill forms through a simple email link in their browser. No downloads or special software required.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use enterprise-grade encryption and comply with data protection regulations. All data flows directly to your CMS.

Can multiple lawyers use it?

Absolutely. Assign leads to specific lawyers, track progress by team member, and scale your intake across your entire firm.

What if I need help?

We provide onboarding support and are available via email/chat during business hours. Our team is here to help you succeed.
